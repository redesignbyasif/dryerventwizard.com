GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We've got answers.

When it comes to your dryer vent, a little knowledge goes a long way.

What are the signs my dryer vent needs cleaning?+

Longer drying times, a dryer that feels hot, musty clothes, or excess lint can all be warning signs. A professional inspection can help identify the issue.

How often should my dryer vent be cleaned?+

Dryer manufacturers and the Consumer Product Safety Commission recommend servicing your dryer and vent at least once a year. Your needs may vary with usage.

Can I get an exact price over the phone?+

An in-person assessment helps your service professional provide an accurate quote before work begins.
